Vidala · Isadora Duncan Dancer

Among Isadora Duncan’s handpicked stars was Lily Dikovskaya, appointed by Isadora as her dance captain and sole authority to pass on her performance legacy to the next generation of Isadora Duncan Dancers.

Lily’s sole protégée is Vidala—acclaimed by The New York Times, Newsday, and Celebrated Artists, and hailed internationally as “Isadora’s Heir.”
